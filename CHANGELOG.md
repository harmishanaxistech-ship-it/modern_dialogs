## 1.0.0

* Initial release of Modern Dialogs.
* Built-in dialog types: Success, Error, Warning, Info, Confirm, Loading, Custom.
* Support for Fade, Scale, Slide, and Bounce animations.
* Global access via `navigatorKey`.
* Highly customizable UI with Material 3 support.
* Dark and Light theme support.
* Background blur (Glassmorphism) effect.
